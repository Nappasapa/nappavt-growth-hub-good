Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026


This Privacy Policy describes how NappaVT Growth Hub handles information when connected to creator accounts such as Twitch, YouTube and TikTok.


1. Information used

The Growth Hub may process account identifiers, public profile information, video metadata, stream metadata and performance statistics made available through authorized platform APIs.


2. TikTok information

When TikTok access is authorized, the Growth Hub may request read-only permissions such as user.info.basic and video.list to identify the connected account, list public videos and retrieve available video statistics.


3. How information is used

Information is used to match published videos with the creator's content queue, display analytics, keep historical performance snapshots and compare content performance over time.


4. What the Growth Hub does not do

The Growth Hub does not sell personal information, does not use connected account data for advertising and does not automatically publish, delete or modify TikTok or YouTube content.


5. Storage

Authorization tokens and dashboard data may be stored on the private backend used by the Growth Hub so authorized analytics can refresh automatically. Access credentials are not intentionally exposed in the public website.


6. Sharing

Connected-platform information is not intentionally shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data is transmitted only as needed between the Growth Hub and the official APIs or infrastructure used to operate the service.


7. Revoking access and deletion

The user can revoke a connected platform's access through that platform's account settings. Stored Growth Hub connection data can also be removed by the service administrator when access is no longer required.


8. Third-party services

Twitch, Google/YouTube and TikTok operate independently and process information according to their own privacy policies.


9. Changes

This policy may be updated when integrations or data handling change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
